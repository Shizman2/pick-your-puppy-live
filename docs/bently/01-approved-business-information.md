HEALTH GUARANTEE (FAQ)
- Every puppy comes with a written Health Guarantee.
- The buyer should have the puppy examined by a licensed veterinarian within 10 days of purchase.
- If within 10 days a vet determines in writing that the puppy is clinically ill, or within 30 days that it has a congenital or hereditary defect affecting its health, the buyer may return the puppy for a full refund, return it for a replacement puppy of equal value, or keep it and be reimbursed for reasonable vet fees up to the purchase price.
- The buyer must notify us within 2 business days of the vet's written certification.
- Full terms, conditions, and exclusions are in the Health Guarantee the buyer receives at purchase. For anything beyond this summary, or any specific claim, the customer should contact our team.
